About this page

A short pilot curation for Korea University BUSS215-06·07 (English-taught), Prof. KyungBae Park. Students starting a group project can treat this page as a worked example of selecting, labeling, and presenting public data.

Selection funnel

HackerTube open list 6,454→ AI area 576→ Generative-AI pilot 0→ Reviewed cards 0

Score used for ranking: rec_months + 0.5 × strong-recommendation months. News items and a short exclusion list are dropped; evergreen titles are preferred.

How this was built

1. Data

Public HackerTube dataset (Andrej Karpathy): ~43M Hacker News comments (2007–2026) scanned for YouTube links. A comment counts only with 100+ characters of its own text. Kept videos mentioned by at least 3 different people in at least 3 different months → about 6,454 videos under ODC-By 1.0.

2. Our selection rule for this pilot

select.py keeps AI-area titles (llms-generative-ai, or machine-learning with generative-AI keywords), drops news shelf-life / news content-type and a fixed exclusion set, then sorts evergreen first and by score descending. Topics are assigned with keyword rules into five course-facing groups.

3. What AI did vs what humans decided

  • AI: comment labeling upstream, English summaries / why-lines in the source cards, and draft three-line card text.
  • Humans: pilot scope, naming and privacy rules (no HN user IDs), what to drop, review of the finished cards, and course framing.

4. Try it in your project

BUSS215-06·07 example: the team kept one folder as the source of truth. AGENTS.md (auto-read) sets the rules; the Core 5 files hold the map, why, done-means, now-tasks, and history.

In Antigravity: first setup → AGENTS.md → P0–P5 (scope → requirements → create 6 files + 4 folders → verify → first task → continue next day in a new chat). Each work day: Start session (read 00_HOME → 03_TASKS → latest 04_WORKLOG entry) → work (check the diff before saving) → End session (log unfinished items and why).

Big choices are short ADRs in decisions/. Tests live in evidence/. Agents draft; humans approve scope and anything shared.

Open the files:
